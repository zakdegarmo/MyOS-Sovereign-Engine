22. Logic :: Governs (Thought)
Who is it? The Editor. The Censor. The Traffic Controller. It is the police officer standing in the intersection of the mind, directing which thoughts are valid and which are jaywalking.
What is it? The Regulation of Flow. Thoughts can be wild and associative. Logic governs them by imposing syntax, grammar, and reason. It turns a "brainstorm" into an "argument."
When is it? During critical thinking, writing, debate, and coding. When you say, "Wait, that doesn't make sense."
Where is it? In the Prefrontal Cortex. It sits in the executive center, filtering the noise from the impulsive lower brain.
Why is it? For Accuracy and Utility. Ungoverned thought is dreaming. Governed thought is problem-solving. It ensures that mental energy is directed efficiently towards a conclusion.
How does it operate? Through Inference and Deduction. It creates chains of cause and effect. "If A is true, then B must be true." It prunes branches of thought that lead to dead ends.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Rational Governance. It establishes the rule of law within the mind, preventing cognitive anarchy and allowing for complex, multi-step intellectual feats.
