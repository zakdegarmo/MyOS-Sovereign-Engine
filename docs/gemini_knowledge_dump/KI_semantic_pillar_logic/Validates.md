26. Logic :: Validates (Improvement)
Who is it? The Auditor. The QA Tester. The Scoreboard. It is the cold, hard number that tells you if you actually got better or if you're just deluding yourself.
What is it? The Measurement of Progress. Improvement requires a "better" and a "worse." Logic defines the metric. It turns "I feel stronger" into "I lifted 5kg more."
When is it? During performance reviews, testing, debugging, and benchmarking.
Where is it? In the Data. It sits in the spreadsheet, the analytics dashboard, and the test results.
Why is it? To Prevent Regression and Illusion. Without logical validation, "improvement" is just a feeling. Logic proves it. It separates placebo from cure.
How does it operate? Through Comparison and Metrics. It compares State A (Before) with State B (After) using a consistent standard to calculate the delta.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Empirical Verification. It anchors the concept of growth to objective reality, ensuring that evolution is actual and not merely perceived.
