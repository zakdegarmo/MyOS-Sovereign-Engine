45. Existence :: Is (Existence)
Who is it? The Tautology. The Fact. The I Am That I Am.
What is it? The Act of Being. This is the most fundamental definition. Existence is. It doesn't do anything else; it doesn't need to. It is the state of presence.
When is it? NOW. Existence only happens in the present tense. The past is a memory; the future is a guess. Existence is the eternal Now.
Where is it? HERE. Wherever you are, that is where Existence is happening. It creates the coordinates of location.
Why is it? No Reason. This is the brute fact. Asking "Why is Existence?" implies something outside of Existence caused it, which is impossible. It is the uncaused cause.
How does it operate? Through Presence. It operates by simply occupying space and time. It refuses to be Nothing.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Ontological Presence. It is the simple, crushing, beautiful fact that there is a World, and you are in it. It is the opposite of the Void.
