41. Existence :: Manifests In (Self)
Who is it? The Vessel. The Host. The Avatar. The Self is the local address where Existence has parked itself for a few decades.
What is it? The Localization of Being. Existence is a vast, abstract ocean. The Self is a cup dipped into that ocean. "Manifests In" means that the universal quality of "Is-ness" takes on a specific name, face, and history.
When is it? From the moment of conception to the moment of death. It is the duration of the "I Am."
Where is it? In the Biological Organism. It is located in the cells, the DNA, and the synaptic firing patterns of a specific human being.
Why is it? To Experience Itself. Pure Existence is blind. By manifesting in a Self, Existence gains eyes to see and a mind to know. It creates a point of view.
How does it operate? Through Embodiment. It operates by condensing energy into matter, and matter into consciousness. It creates a boundary (skin/ego) so that "Here" can be distinguished from "There."
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Incarnation. It is the process by which the universal potential becomes a particular actual. It is the mystery of how "Life" becomes "My Life."
