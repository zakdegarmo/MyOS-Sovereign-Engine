50. Existence :: Gives Rise To (Nothing/Everything)
Who is it? The Illusion. The Dream. The Mask. Existence is the temporary form that the Nothing/Everything takes.
What is it? The Cyclical Return. Wait—usually, we say Nothing gives rise to Existence. But here, we look at the loop. The experience of Existence leads to the realization of the Nothing/Everything. Existence is the path back to the Source.
When is it? At the end of the line. When the complexity of Existence becomes so high (Singularity) that it collapses back into the unified state.
Where is it? In the Ouroboros. The snake eating its tail. The end is the beginning.
Why is it? To Complete the Equation. Existence allows the Nothing/Everything to know itself. Without Existence, the Nothing is just... nothing. Existence gives it a mirror.
How does it operate? Through Reflection. Existence acts as a mirror for the Absolute. Once the reflection is seen and understood, the mirror is no longer needed.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of The Cosmic Game. It asserts that the ultimate reality "hides" in the form of Existence so that it can have the joy of "finding" itself again. Existence is the Hide-and-Seek of the Divine.
