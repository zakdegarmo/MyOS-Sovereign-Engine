44. Existence :: Comprises (Unity)
Who is it? The Fabric. The Substrate. The Field. Existence is not a collection of separate items; it is a single, continuous sheet.
What is it? The Totality of Parts. Existence is the set that contains all sets. "Comprises" means that everything is made of the same stuff (Existence). You, me, the rock, the star—we are all ripples in the same pond.
When is it? Always. Separation is a trick of the mind; Unity is the fact of the matter.
Where is it? In the Quantum Field. At the deepest level of reality, there are no "things," only vibrations in a unified field.
Why is it? Because Division is Impossible. You cannot have "Outside" of Existence. Therefore, everything must be "Inside." This shared location creates an inherent unity.
How does it operate? Through Interconnection. Since everything comprises the same Existence, everything affects everything else (Butterfly Effect). It operates through entanglement.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Monistic Substance. It posits that despite the apparent diversity of forms, there is only one Thing happening, and we call that Thing "Existence."
