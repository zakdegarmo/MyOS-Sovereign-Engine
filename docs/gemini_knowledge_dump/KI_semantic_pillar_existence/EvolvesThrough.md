46. Existence :: Evolves Through (Improvement)
Who is it? The River. The Algorithm. The Life Force. Existence is not a static rock; it is a growing tree.
What is it? The Trajectory of Complexity. Over time, Existence tends to organize itself into more complex, capable, and conscious forms. From dust to stars to cells to minds. "Evolves Through" means improvement is the mechanism of its motion.
When is it? Over billions of years (Deep Time). From the Big Bang to the Heat Death (or Singularity).
Where is it? In the Biosphere and Noosphere. It happens in the genetic code of species and the cultural code of civilizations.
Why is it? To Combat Entropy. The universe naturally decays (Entropy). Evolution (Improvement) is the counter-force that builds order (Negentropy). Existence evolves to stay alive.
How does it operate? Through Selection and Iteration. It tries a billion forms. The ones that don't work die. The ones that work survive and reproduce. Existence learns from its mistakes.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Cosmic Meliorism. It suggests that the universe has a direction—it is going somewhere, and that somewhere is "Up" (towards higher complexity and consciousness).
