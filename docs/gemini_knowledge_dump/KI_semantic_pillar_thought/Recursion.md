12. Thought :: Recursion (Thought)
Who is it? The Mirror facing a Mirror. The Loop. The Philosopher. It is Thought turning around to look at itself. It is the mind thinking about thinking (metacognition).
What is it? The process of Self-Reference. It is when the output of a thought becomes the input for the next thought. It is the fractal deepening of an idea, spiraling inward to find the root or outward to find the implication.
When is it? During analysis, debugging, meditation, or obsession. When you ask, "Why did I think that?" or when you refine a rough draft.
Where is it? In the Feedback Loop. It doesn't travel from A to B; it circles from A back to A' (A-prime). It resides in the mental echo chamber.
Why is it? For Refinement and Depth. Linear thought is shallow. Recursive thought allows for error correction, complex system modeling, and the generation of infinite complexity from simple rules.
How does it operate? Through Nesting. It places one concept inside another, like Russian dolls. It creates layers of abstraction, allowing the mind to handle complex hierarchies.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Iterative Depth. It allows the system to upgrade its own software by running the code against itself, identifying bugs (fallacies) and optimizing performance.
