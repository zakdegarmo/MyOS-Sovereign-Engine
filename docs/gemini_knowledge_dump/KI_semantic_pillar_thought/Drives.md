16. Thought :: Drives (Improvement)
Who is it? The Visionary. The Critic. The Navigator. It is the thought that says, "This could be better." It is the mental image of a future that is superior to the present.
What is it? The Spark of Progress. Action follows thought. Before you build a better house, you must think of a better house. Thought provides the vector and direction for improvement.
When is it? When you feel dissatisfaction with the status quo and imagine a solution. During brainstorming, planning, and goal-setting.
Where is it? In the Gap between Now and Then. It lives in the tension between the current reality and the imagined ideal.
Why is it? For Optimization. Biology evolves by chance; minds evolve by intent. Thought speeds up improvement by simulating potential futures and choosing the best one to pursue.
How does it operate? Through Simulation and Incentive. It visualizes the reward of the improved state, generating the dopamine and motivation required to do the work.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Teleological Motivation. It is the mechanism by which the mental realm influences the physical realm, pulling the present forward into a designed future.
