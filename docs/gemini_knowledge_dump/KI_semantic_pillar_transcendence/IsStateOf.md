84. Transcendence :: Is State Of (Unity)
Who is it? The Bliss. The Nirvana. The Union. It is the feeling of being home.
What is it? The Dissolution of Boundaries. Unity is the structure of connection; Transcendence is the state of being inside that connection. It is the experiential reality of Oneness. It is the drop falling into the ocean.
When is it? When the distinction between "Me" and "You" vanishes. When the observer becomes the observed.
Where is it? In the Field. It is a non-local state.
Why is it? To End Isolation. The separate self is lonely and afraid. Transcendence ends the separation. It is the ultimate cure for existential angst.
How does it operate? Through Fusion. It melts the walls of the ego. It operates like heat turning ice (solid/separate) into water (fluid/unified).
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Mystical Union. It is the core experience at the heart of all major spiritual traditions—the realization that we are not separate waves, but the ocean itself.
