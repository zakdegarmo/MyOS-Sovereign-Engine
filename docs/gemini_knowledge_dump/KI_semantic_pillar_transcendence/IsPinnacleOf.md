87. Transcendence :: Is Pinnacle Of (Mastery)
Who is it? The Wizard. The Sage. The Source. The Master stops playing the instrument and becomes the music.
What is it? The Effortless Action. Mastery implies control. Transcendence implies surrender. The highest level of Mastery is when you are so good you don't have to control it anymore; you let it flow through you. You transcend the technique.
When is it? When the dancer disappears. When the archer hits the target with their eyes closed.
Where is it? Beyond the 10,000 Hours. It is the 10,001st hour.
Why is it? To Return to Innocence. The novice is unconscious. The master is conscious. The transcendent master is "Unconsciously Conscious." They return to the simplicity of the child, but with the power of the master.
How does it operate? Through Wu Wei (Non-Doing). It operates by getting the self out of the way so the Tao can act.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Artless Art. It is the Zen concept that the ultimate skill is no-skill. It is the point where human effort merges with divine grace.
