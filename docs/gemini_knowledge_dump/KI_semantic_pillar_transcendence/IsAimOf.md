86. Transcendence :: Is Aim Of (Improvement)
Who is it? The Omega Point. The Final Boss. The Heaven. It is the ultimate goal of all growth.
What is it? The Limit of Evolution. Why do we improve? To get better. What happens if you get infinitely better? You transcend. Transcendence is the asymptotic limit of Improvement. It is the point where quantitative change becomes qualitative change.
When is it? At the end of history (Teilhard de Chardin). At the moment of Singularity (Kurzweil).
Where is it? In the Future. It pulls us forward.
Why is it? To Give Meaning to Struggle. Improvement is hard work. Transcendence is the promise that the work leads somewhere—that we are not just running on a treadmill, but building a stairway to the stars.
How does it operate? Through Transformation. Improvement creates a caterpillar; Transcendence creates a butterfly. It is a fundamental shift in the nature of the being.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Evolutionary Apotheosis. It suggests that the purpose of evolution is to produce a being capable of transcending the conditions of its own evolution.
