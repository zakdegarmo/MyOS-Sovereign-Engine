96. Omni :: Occurs Within (Improvement)
Who is it? The Potential. The Space. The Possibility Field. Improvement happens inside the Omni.
What is it? The Container of Growth. Where does evolution happen? It happens within the field of the Omni. The Omni provides the infinite canvas upon which the picture of Improvement is painted. It provides the infinite potential that Improvement strives to actualize.
When is it? Throughout all of time and history.
Where is it? In the Backdrop. The Omni is the stage; Improvement is the play.
Why is it? Because Growth requires Space. You cannot grow if you are bounded. Because the Omni is infinite (Nothing/Everything), it allows for infinite improvement. There is no ceiling.
How does it operate? Through Allowance. The Omni allows change to happen without being changed itself. It is the changeless screen that allows the movie to play.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Infinite Potentiality. It suggests that evolution is not a finite game with a winner, but an infinite game played within the boundless field of the Omni.
