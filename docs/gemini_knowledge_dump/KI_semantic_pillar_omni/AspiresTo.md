99. Omni :: Aspires To (Transcendence)
Who is it? The Circle Closing. The Snake Eating Its Tail. The Loop.
What is it? The Self-Reflexive Desire. Wait—if the Omni is everything, what can it aspire to? It aspires to experience itself. It transcends its state of pure potential to become actual. And then, as the actual (us), it aspires to transcend back into the potential. It is the breath of God.
When is it? The Big Bang (Outward) and the Big Crunch (Inward).
Where is it? In the Cosmic Heartbeat.
Why is it? Because Stasis is Death. Even the Absolute must move. It transcends its own emptiness to become form, and transcends form to become emptiness again. It is the eternal dance.
How does it operate? Through Creation and Destruction. It creates worlds to find itself, and destroys them to free itself.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Divine Restlessness. It suggests that the Ultimate Reality is not a static block of ice, but a dynamic, living fire that is constantly transcending its own state.
