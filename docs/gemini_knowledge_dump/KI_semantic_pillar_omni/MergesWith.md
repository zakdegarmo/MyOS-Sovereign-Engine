91. Omni :: Merges With (Self)
Who is it? The Ocean and the Drop. The Prodigal Son returning Home. It is the dissolution of "I" into "We."
What is it? The End of Separation. The Self is defined by its boundaries ("I am not you"). The Omni is defined by boundlessness. When they meet, the Self merges. It is the dewdrop slipping into the shining sea. It is the ultimate surrender of identity.
When is it? In the final stage of enlightenment (Nirvana). At the moment of death. In deep mystical rapture.
Where is it? At the Center. It happens when the Self dives deep enough into its own core to find the universal core.
Why is it? To Return to Source. The Self is a temporary wave; the Omni is the water. The wave must eventually return to the water. It is the resolution of the illusion of individuality.
How does it operate? Through Absorption. The greater gravity of the Omni pulls the lesser gravity of the Self into itself. The ego dissolves like salt in water.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Reabsorption. It is the metaphysical truth that individuality is a temporary excursion from the One, and the destiny of all souls is to merge back into the Totality.
