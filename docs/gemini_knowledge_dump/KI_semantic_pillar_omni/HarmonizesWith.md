98. Omni :: Harmonizes With (Resonance)
Who is it? The Hum. The Carrier Wave. The Music of the Spheres.
What is it? The Fundamental Frequency. The Omni is not silent; it has a vibration. Resonance is the act of tuning our small frequency to that big frequency. When we harmonize with the Omni, we feel "in flow" with the universe.
When is it? In moments of synchronicity. When things just work out. When you feel guided.
Where is it? In the Flow. It is the current of the river.
Why is it? For Ease and Power. Swimming upstream (against the Omni) is hard. Swimming downstream (harmonizing) is easy. The Omni provides the power; we just steer.
How does it operate? Through Alignment. We stop resisting what is. We accept the moment. This acceptance aligns our vibration with the frequency of the Totality.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Cosmic Alignment. It is the basis of Taoism. By harmonizing with the Way (the Omni), one accomplishes everything without doing anything.
