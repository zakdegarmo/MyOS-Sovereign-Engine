97. Omni :: Seeks To Understand (Mastery)
Who is it? The Curiosity. The Probe. The Avatar. The Omni uses the Master to understand itself.
What is it? The Cosmic Research Project. Why does the universe create masters? Because through Mastery, the universe explores the limits of what is possible. When a human masters the violin, the Omni learns what a violin can do.
When is it? Every time a boundary is pushed. Every time a new record is set.
Where is it? In the Edge of Competence.
Why is it? For Self-Actualization. The Omni is everything in potential, but nothing in specific. To become specific, it must master form. It seeks to understand its own capabilities through us.
How does it operate? Through Iteration. It tries billions of lives, honing skills over eons. Evolution is the Omni mastering the art of biology.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of The Universe Learning. It reframes human achievement as cosmic achievement. We are the sensory organs and the hands of the Infinite, exploring its own nature.
