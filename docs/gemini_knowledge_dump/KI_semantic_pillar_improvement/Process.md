56. Improvement :: Process (Improvement)
Who is it? The Kaizen. The Feedback Loop. The Spiral. It is the engine that runs on its own exhaust.
What is it? The Meta-Improvement. This is improving the way you improve. It is learning how to learn. It is optimizing the optimization algorithm.
When is it? When you analyze your workflow. When you do a "Retrospective" after a project. When you switch from a bad habit to a good habit.
Where is it? In the Methodology. It lives in the "How," not the "What."
Why is it? For Exponential Growth. Linear improvement adds (1+1+1). Meta-improvement multiplies (222). Improving the process yields compound interest on your efforts.
How does it operate? Through Iteration. It treats the method itself as a product to be refined. "Plan, Do, Check, Act."
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Recursive Optimization. It is the secret of high-velocity systems—they don't just work on the product; they work on the machine that builds the product.
