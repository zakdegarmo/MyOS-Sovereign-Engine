4. Self :: Seeks
Who is it?
The Lover. The Tribe Member. The Orphan. It is the part of the Self that feels an innate incompleteness, a fundamental sense of separation that drives it to connect with something outside of itself.
What is it?
The Gravitational Pull towards wholeness. It is the primal desire to dissolve the boundary between "self" and "other," whether that other is a person, a group, a god, or the cosmos. It is the opposite of alienation.
When is it?
It is a constant, low-level hum that becomes loud during times of loneliness, love, grief, or spiritual yearning. It's the driving force behind forming families, communities, and religions.
Where is it?
In the Heart (metaphorically). It lives in the emotional bonds between people, in the feeling of belonging, and in the quiet moments when one feels connected to all of existence.
Why is it?
For Survival and Meaning. Survival, because there is strength in numbers. Meaning, because a Self that is part of a larger whole has a purpose beyond its own finite existence.
How does it operate?
Through Empathy, Communication, and Vulnerability. It works by lowering the defenses of the Ego to allow for genuine connection, shared experience, and mutual support.
Ontological Phenomena Overview
This is the phenomenon of Cohesive Attraction. It reveals that the state of being a separate Self is inherently unstable and that a fundamental law of consciousness is the drive to merge and form more complex, unified wholes.
