9. Self :: Aspires To
Who is it?
The Seeker. The Mystic. The Pilgrim. It is the part of the Self that looks up and out, feeling the limitations of its own identity and longing to go beyond them.
What is it?
The Upward Vector of Desire. It is the innate yearning for liberation, for meaning, for connection with the infinite. It is the desire to break free from the cage of the ego, the mundane, and mortality.
When is it?
It is felt most strongly in moments of awe before the vastness of nature, when contemplating deep philosophical questions, during profound suffering, or in acts of selfless love and creativity.
Where is it?
In the Spirit. It is not located in thought or emotion alone, but in the deepest, most essential part of one's being that intuits a reality greater than itself.
Why is it?
To Complete the Journey. The Self feels itself to be a fragment of a larger whole, and this aspiration is the homing instinct to return to that source, to find ultimate meaning and resolve the paradox of being a finite creature in an infinite universe.
How does it operate?
Through Imagination, Faith, and Peak Experiences. It works by allowing the Self to conceive of and feel a connection to states of being that are beyond its current direct experience, pulling it forward and upward.
Ontological Phenomena Overview
This is the phenomenon of Escape Velocity. The Self is a system held in orbit by the gravity of its own limitations, but it possesses an inherent engine of aspiration powerful enough to break free and journey into a higher, more expansive state of consciousness.
