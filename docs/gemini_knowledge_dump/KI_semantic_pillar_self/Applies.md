3. Self :: Applies
Who is it?
The Strategist. The Decision-Maker. The Judge. This is the part of the Self that takes the cold, abstract rules of Logic and puts them to work in the real world. It is the user of the tool.
What is it?
The act of Execution. It's the bridge between the theoretical world of "if-then" and the practical world of "what do I do now?" The Self applies Logic to filter its impulses, plan its moves, and build its arguments.
When is it?
During any moment of non-instinctual choice. When you're budgeting, planning a trip, debugging code, trying to win an argument, or simply deciding the most efficient route to the store.
Where is it?
In the Deliberative Mind. It resides in the mental space where options are weighed and consequences are simulated before an action is taken.
Why is it?
For Effectiveness. The Self applies Logic to increase its chances of survival and success. Random or emotional choices can be catastrophic. Logical choices, while not always perfect, are optimized for predictable, favorable outcomes.
How does it operate?
By taking a goal, assessing the available data, and running it through a framework of rules to produce a decision or a plan. It is the engine that turns raw data into a reasoned course of action.
Ontological Phenomena Overview
This is the phenomenon of Rational Agency. The Self is not merely a passive entity; it is an agent that can actively wield abstract systems (like Logic) to manipulate itself and its environment to achieve its goals.
