# Self :: Is Realized By

Who is it?
Paradoxically, No One. It is the Self at the moment it recognizes its own illusory nature. It is the Witness at the moment it realizes it is witnessing itself.
What is it?
The Ultimate Realization. It is the collapsing of the subject/object duality. The Self finally understands that its separate, solid identity is a construct ("Nothing"), and that its true nature is inseparable from the entire fabric of existence ("Everything").
When is it?
In the singular, timeless moment of Enlightenment or Satori. It is not something the Self achieves, but something that happens when the Self gets out of the way. It is a seeing-through of the illusion, not the construction of a new belief.
Where is it?
Nowhere and Everywhere. At this point of realization, the Self understands it was never located "in here" looking "out there." Its true location is the entire field of being itself.
Why is it?
To End Suffering. The belief in a separate, vulnerable Self is the root of fear, craving, and conflict. The realization of its true nature as Nothing/Everything is the ultimate liberation from this suffering.
How does it operate?
Through the Complete Surrender of the Ego. It is not something the Self achieves, but something that happens when the Self gets out of the way. It is a seeing-through of the illusion, not the construction of a new belief.
Ontological Phenomena Overview
This is the phenomenon of Paradoxical Resolution. The entire complex structure of the Self, with all its striving and seeking, is ultimately a bootstrap program designed to lead to its own dissolution, revealing the un-programmed, foundational reality that was present all along.
