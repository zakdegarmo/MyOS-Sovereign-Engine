71. Resonance :: Is Felt By (Self)
Who is it? The Sensor. The Antenna. The Heart. The Self acts as the instrument that detects the vibration.
What is it? The Subjective Registration. Resonance is physics, but it means nothing until it is felt. "Is Felt By" describes the moment the external wave hits the internal shore. It is the translation of energy into emotion or sensation.
When is it? In moments of intuition, empathy, or aesthetic arrest. When you say, "That speaks to me."
Where is it? In the Nervous System and Soul. It happens in the gut (instinct), the chest (emotion), and the skin (goosebumps).
Why is it? For Navigation. We use resonance to navigate the world. We move towards things that resonate positively (attraction) and away from things that resonate negatively (dissonance). It is our internal radar.
How does it operate? Through Sympathy. The Self vibrates at the same frequency as the stimulus. Like a tuning fork reacting to a piano note.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Sentient Reception. It frames the Self not as an isolated island, but as a responsive node in a web of vibrations, capable of detecting truth and intent through feeling.
