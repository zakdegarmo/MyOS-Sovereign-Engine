69. Mastery :: Approaches (Transcendence)
Who is it? The Edge. The Threshold. The Event Horizon. Mastery takes you to the very end of what is humanly possible.
What is it? The Bridge to the Divine. You cannot force Transcendence, but Mastery gets you close enough to jump. It is the "Approaching" of the limit. Perfect form dissolves into formlessness.
When is it? In the final stages of the craft. When the martial artist stops fighting and starts flowing. When the painter paints with empty space.
Where is it? At the Boundary. The line between the Doing and the Being.
Why is it? Because Finite leads to Infinite. By perfecting the finite form, the master exhausts the possibilities of the form and is forced to look beyond it.
How does it operate? Through Refinement. You polish the mirror until the dust is gone. When the dust is gone, you don't see the mirror; you see the Light (Transcendence) reflected in it.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Asymptotic Divinity. It suggests that if you do anything perfectly enough, it becomes a spiritual act. Excellence is a gateway to the Holy.
