65. Mastery :: Commands (Existence)
Who is it? The Magician. The Commander. The Creator. It is the one who bends reality to their will.
What is it? The Exertion of Will over Matter. Mastery gives you leverage. A master can make Existence do things it wouldn't do on its own. They shape stone, direct sound, cure bodies, and build empires. They command the resources of reality.
When is it? When the conductor raises the baton and the silence breaks. When the surgeon creates order from biological chaos.
Where is it? In the Zone of Influence. It is the sphere of reality that falls under the master's control.
Why is it? For Creation. Existence is raw potential. Mastery is the organizing principle. Without command, existence is wild. Mastery tames it to create civilization and art.
How does it operate? Through Authority and Skill. The master knows the laws of Existence better than anyone else, which gives them the authority to exploit those laws. You command nature by obeying it perfectly.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Sovereign Agency. It represents the shift from being a victim of circumstance to being a co-creator of reality.
