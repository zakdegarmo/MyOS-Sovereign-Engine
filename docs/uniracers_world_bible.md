# UNIRACERS WORLD: DESIGN BIBLE & CANONICAL MASTER LATTICE
*GTA Vice City on One Wheel — The Definitive Production Document*

## 1. Core Identity & Philosophy
* **Tagline**: *"GTA Vice City on One Wheel."*
* **Heritage**: DMA Design (1994) irreverent kinetic momentum meets hardboiled neon neo-noir.
* **Ludonarrative Law**: The game has to help tell the story, and the story has to make the game fun. Stunts are not just points; stunts build pneumatic turbo pressure required to outrun corporate militarized enforcers.
* **Dialogue Rule**: **Zero spoken dialogue during physical gameplay.** Characters communicate via silent mechanical pantomime, tire screeches, frame tilts, and 10W-40 squirts. The narrative is delivered exclusively via Red's hardboiled Raymond Chandler-style internal monologue voiceover and environmental radio broadcasts.

---

## 2. The Triadic Mode Architecture (3 Games in 1)

### A. Free Roam Sandbox (The World Torus Knot)
* **Topology**: A continuous $(2, 3)$ Trefoil Torus Knot spline manifold bounded in a $5000\text{m} \times 5000\text{m} \times 5000\text{m}$ cube ($26.85\text{ km}$ total centerline loop).
* **The World IS the Track**: The entire world is a multi-lane unicycle highway with zero dead ends and zero boundary walls.
* **Fractal Hierarchy**:
  * **World Scale**: Continuous 3-lane highway loop connecting 10 Metropolises.
  * **City Scale**: 2-lane beltways and cloverleaf flyovers surrounding each city.
  * **Town/District Scale**: 1-lane utility roads connecting 10 Towns per city (**100 total race tracks** worldwide).
  * **Interior Architecture**: Buildings are traversable internally via spiral stairwells, HVAC ventilation ramps, and elevator shafts designed specifically for unicycles.
* **Camera Freedom**: Player can freely toggle between 3D orbital chase and locked 2D retro side-scroll.

### B. Story Mode (Hardboiled Noir)
* **Directed Perspectives**: Forced cinematic camera angles specific to story beats (Dutch angles in alleys, high-span crane shots, sudden locks into 2D side-scrolling obstacle courses during high-speed tunnel chases).
* **Narrative Progression**: Story beats seamlessly lead into themed gameplay trials, pursuits, and boss duels.

### C. Tournament Mode (Pure Deterministic Mastery)
* **The 2D Classic Circuit**: Camera locked perpendicular to the unicycle, recreating 100% authentic 1994 SNES *Unirally/Uniracers* side-scrolling physics, twitch stunts, and flat landing momentum.
* **The 3D Speed Circuit**: Dynamic third-person multi-lane drafting and bank-turn racing.
* **The 4D Singularity Circuit**: Topological Klein bottle / Tesseract manifold tracks.

## 3. Act 1: Canonical Story Arc & Production Beats

### Beat 1: Intro Cutscene — "The Scrap Yard Awakening" *(FILMED)*
* **File**: `apps/web/public/screenings/act1_scrap_yard_awakening.mp4`
* **Plot**: Red returns to his solitary scrap yard garage. Reclining on his beaten couch with a can of 10W-40, watching CRT TV nostalgia of the 1994 glory days.
* **The Catalyst**: At the very end of the cutscene, a sudden, heavy crash/noise echoes outside in the scrap yard.

### Beat 2: Tutorial Level — "The Salvage Investigation"
* **Setting**: Red's industrial scrap yard hangout.
* **Objective**: Red rolls outside into the salvage yard to investigate the noise.
* **Tutorial Progression (Red's Noir Narration as Guide)**:
  1. *Locomotion & Cadence*: Holding `W` / RT to build forward torque and balance cadence.
  2. *Gyroscopic Lean*: `A` / `D` or Left Stick to bank into curved surfaces and initiate drift.
  3. *Obstacle Bunny Hop*: Tapping `Space` / `A` button to hop over rusted copper piping and girders.
  4. *Salvage Kicker Stunts*: Launching off `junkyard_kicker_1`, pulling a 360 twist/backflip, and landing flat to ignite the pneumatic turbo boost.
* **The Finish Line**: Red clears the final scrap pile and discovers **Green** mangled in the junk (flat tire, cracked hub).

### Beat 3: Second Cutscene — "The Confession & The Ambush"
* **Staging**: Red tows Green into his garage on a workshop dolly and fixes her tire with fresh rim tape and 10W-40.
* **Red's Monologue & Revelations**:
  * Green reveals she is part of an underground resistance network actively sabotaging **CorpoCycle**.
  * **The Lore**: CorpoCycle is the greedy conglomerate that slapped the league with a corporate injunction in 1994—the same company responsible for the popular 1987 unicycle cartoon (*Red's Dream*) that bore an uncanny resemblance to Red.
  * **The Gut-Punch**: Green reveals that **Blue** (Red's best friend and brother-in-arms from the 90s) was working alongside her in the resistance and has recently gone **MISSING**. Red is gutted; his cynicism shatters into personal resolve.
* **The Ambush**: Harsh spotlights cut through the garage windows. The sinister **Uni-Nators** have tracked Green.
* **The Diversion**: Red tells Green to stay hidden while he screech-burns out into the streets to lead the enforcers away.

### Beat 4: Level 1 — "The Outlaw Pursuit"
* **Setting**: High-velocity city streets and industrial beltways.
* **Gameplay**: Red leads the Uni-Nators on a multi-lap pursuit through the city.
* **Mechanics**:
  * Red must build turbo through stunt jumps while avoiding being boxed in or rammed by the heavy pursuit cycles.
  * Quicktime route choices (e.g. branch into an underground transit tube vs a high-span bridge).
  * Objective: Survive and build enough distance over the laps to completely lose the tail.
