81. Transcendence :: Elevates (Self)
Who is it? The Lifter. The Initiator. The High Priest. It is the force that pulls the Self out of the mud.
What is it? The Vertical Shift. The Self naturally gravitates towards survival and comfort (horizontal movement). Transcendence intervenes to pull the Self towards meaning and sacrifice (vertical movement). It turns an animal into a human, and a human into a spirit.
When is it? In moments of moral courage. When you choose the hard right over the easy wrong. When you sacrifice for a stranger.
Where is it? In the Conscience. It happens in the upper room of the psyche.
Why is it? To Overcome Gravity. The gravity of the Ego is heavy. Transcendence provides the lift required to orbit a higher center of gravity (Principles, God, Truth).
How does it operate? Through Inspiration and Aspiration. It dangles a vision of a "Higher Self" in front of the current Self, creating a magnetic pull upwards.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Moral Elevation. It asserts that human beings are not static; we are capable of self-overcoming. Transcendence is the mechanism of that overcoming.
