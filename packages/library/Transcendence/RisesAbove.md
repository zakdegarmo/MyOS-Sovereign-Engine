85. Transcendence :: Rises Above (Existence)
Who is it? The Spirit. The Ghost. The Light. It is the part of us that does not rot.
What is it? The Detach from Materiality. Existence is heavy; it is bound by gravity, time, and decay. Transcendence rises above these constraints. It is the realization that consciousness is not strictly dependent on matter.
When is it? In Out-of-Body Experiences. In deep meditation. In the attitude of the Stoic who is happy even in chains.
Where is it? In the Ether. It occupies a subtle dimension above the physical plane.
Why is it? For Freedom. If we are only Existence, we are slaves to physics and death. Transcendence offers a loophole. It offers a freedom that the world cannot touch.
How does it operate? Through Dis-identification. It operates by shifting the center of identity from the body ("I am this meat") to the witness ("I am the one watching this meat").
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Spiritual Autonomy. It asserts the primacy of Mind over Matter. It is the victory of the internal state over external circumstances.
