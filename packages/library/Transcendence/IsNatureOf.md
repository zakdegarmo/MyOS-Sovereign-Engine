90. Transcendence :: Is Nature Of (Omni)
Who is it? The Beyond. The Infinite. The Alpha and Omega. It is the definition of God.
What is it? The Quality of the Absolute. Why is the Omni (Nothing/Everything) so hard to describe? Because its very nature is Transcendence. It transcends all categories, all names, and all limits. It is "Neti Neti" (Not this, not that).
When is it? Always. It is the "Forever Beyond."
Where is it? Outside the Box. Whatever box you draw, the Omni is outside of it.
Why is it? Because Limits are Created. The Omni is the Uncreated. Therefore, it transcends all limits.
How does it operate? Through Incomprehensibility. It operates by being bigger than the mind that tries to hold it. It defeats the container.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Divine Otherness. It reminds us that no matter how much we know, the Ultimate Reality remains a mystery. It is the horizon that recedes as we approach it.
