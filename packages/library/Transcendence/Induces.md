88. Transcendence :: Induces (Resonance)
Who is it? The Bell. The Thunder. The Revelation. It strikes a chord that shakes the foundations.
What is it? The Vibration of Truth. When you encounter something Transcendent (great art, deep truth, saintly love), it rings in your soul. It induces a powerful resonance called "Awe." It forces you to vibrate at a higher frequency.
When is it? When you cry at a sunset. When you feel the "Holy Spirit." When a speech gives you chills.
Where is it? In the Depth. It resonates in the deepest part of the psyche.
Why is it? To Wake Us Up. We sleepwalk through life. Transcendence shocks us awake. The resonance breaks the shell of our mundane perception.
How does it operate? Through Transmission. The higher frequency overcomes the lower frequency. It is energetic contagion.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Sublime Impact. It explains why we seek out the sacred and the beautiful. We want to be shaken. We want to be tuned to the frequency of the Divine.
