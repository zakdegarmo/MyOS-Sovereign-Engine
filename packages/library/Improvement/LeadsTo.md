57. Improvement :: Leads To (Mastery)
Who is it? The Path. The Staircase. The Journey. Improvement is the steps; Mastery is the view from the top.
What is it? The Accumulation of Gains. You don't jump to Mastery. You improve your way there. "Leads To" implies causality. Continuous, directed improvement inevitably results in Mastery.
When is it? Over the long haul. It is the "Compound Effect." The daily 1% improvement that leads to being 37x better in a year.
Where is it? In the Trajectory. It is the vector pointing from "Novice" to "Expert."
Why is it? Because Mastery is the Limit Function. If you keep improving, eventually you run out of obvious errors to fix. You enter the realm of nuance. That realm is Mastery.
How does it operate? Through Persistence. It operates by refusing to stop at "Good Enough." It pushes past the plateau.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Cumulative Excellence. It demystifies genius, framing it as the mathematical result of sustained, incremental refinement over time.
