59. Improvement :: Is Path To (Transcendence)
Who is it? The Pilgrimage. The Ascent. The Training. You cannot fly until your wings are strong. Improvement strengthens the wings.
What is it? The Preparation for the Leap. Transcendence is a discontinuity (a jump). But you need a solid platform to jump from. Improvement builds that platform. You improve your meditation practice until, one day, you transcend the practice.
When is it? The lifetime of discipline before the moment of enlightenment. The years of study before the breakthrough discovery.
Where is it? On the Mountain Face. It is the climbing. Transcendence is the summit.
Why is it? To Build Capacity. The vessel must be strong enough to hold the light. If you transcend before you have improved (purified) your vessel, you crack (madness). Improvement prepares the structure for the influx of higher energy.
How does it operate? Through Gradual Elevation. It raises the baseline frequency of the system step by step until it is close enough to the higher frequency to bridge the gap.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Developmental Preparation. It suggests that while Grace (Transcendence) may be sudden, it favors the prepared mind (Improvement).
