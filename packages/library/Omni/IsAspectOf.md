94. Omni :: Is Aspect Of (Unity)
Who is it? The Substance. The Content. The Stuff. Unity is the form of connection; Omni is the thing being connected.
What is it? The Material of Oneness. If everything is One (Unity), what is that One made of? It is made of the Omni. The Omni is the clay; Unity is the ball of clay. The Omni is the "Aspect" that allows Unity to exist because it provides the underlying continuity.
When is it? Always.
Where is it? Underneath. It is the substrate.
Why is it? Because You cannot unite unlike things. You can only unite things that share a common nature. The Omni is that common nature. It is the "Is-ness" shared by all things.
How does it operate? Through Permeation. The Omni permeates every atom and every void, ensuring that there are no gaps where Unity could break.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Substantial Monism. It posits that Unity is not just an arrangement of parts, but a revelation that the parts are all made of the same infinite substance.
