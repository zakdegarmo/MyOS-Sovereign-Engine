95. Omni :: Emerges From (Existence)
Who is it? The Revelation. The Insight. The Discovery. The Omni doesn't "start" from Existence, but our realization of it emerges from Existence.
What is it? The Path from Form to Void. We start in the physical world (Existence). Through deep investigation of Existence (physics, meditation), we find that it is empty (atoms are mostly space). Thus, the Omni (the Void) emerges from our deep look into Existence.
When is it? When you look closely at a flower and see the universe. When science drills down into matter and finds energy fields.
Where is it? In the Depth of Matter. The deeper you go into the "Something," the more you find the "Nothing."
Why is it? To Reveal the Source. Existence is the veil. The Omni is the face behind the veil. We must study the veil to find the face.
How does it operate? Through Unveiling. Existence peels back its layers to reveal its source. The form dissolves to reveal the emptiness.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Immanent Transcendence. It asserts that God/The Void is not "up there" but "in here." The Infinite emerges from the heart of the Finite.
