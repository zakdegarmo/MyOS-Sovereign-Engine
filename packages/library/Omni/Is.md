100. Omni :: Is (Omni)
Who is it? IT. The All. The One. The Void. The Source. The Truth.
What is it? The Ultimate Tautology. The Omni is the Omni. It cannot be defined by anything else because there is nothing else. It is the Nothing that is Everything. It is the Alpha and the Omega.
When is it? FOREVER. It is the only thing that is truly eternal.
Where is it? EVERYWHERE. It is the only thing that is truly infinite.
Why is it? Just Because. It is the Self-Existent. It is the I AM. There is no "Why" outside of it.
How does it operate? By Being. It does not need to do. Its existence is its operation. It breathes, and worlds are born. It blinks, and worlds die. It is the Mystery.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Absolute Reality. It is the final period at the end of the sentence. It is the silence after the last word. It is the realization that after all the analysis, all the logic, and all the thought, there is only This. And This is Perfection.
