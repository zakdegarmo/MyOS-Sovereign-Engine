6. Self :: Undergoes
Who is it?
The Student. The Evolver. The Work-in-Progress. The Self is not the one doing the improving in this context; it is the object that is being improved. It is the clay on the potter's wheel.
What is it?
The state of Malleability. It is the capacity of the Self to be changed, updated, and refined over time. It is the process of becoming a better version of itself.
When is it?
Continuously, across the entire lifespan. It happens through learning in childhood, adapting in adulthood, and gaining wisdom in old age. It is the very nature of a dynamic system.
Where is it?
In the Neural Pathways of the brain that rewire with new experiences (neuroplasticity). It is in the Character as new virtues are developed. It is in the Skillset as new abilities are acquired.
Why is it?
For Adaptation. A static Self in a changing world is a dead Self. The ability to undergo improvement is essential to meet new challenges, correct past mistakes, and increase effectiveness.
How does it operate?
Through Feedback Loops. The Self acts, the world provides a consequence, and the Self updates its internal model. It works through trial, error, and integration of new data.
Ontological Phenomena Overview
This is the phenomenon of Dynamic Plasticity. It proves the Self is not a fixed, monolithic entity but a mutable, adaptable system designed for growth and evolution in response to its experiences.
