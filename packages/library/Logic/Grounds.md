29. Logic :: Grounds (Transcendence)
Who is it? The Launchpad. The Tether. The Roots. It is the gravity that keeps the mystic from floating away into madness.
What is it? The Integration of the Divine. Transcendence goes beyond logic, but Logic provides the place to return to. It allows us to articulate the ineffable. It keeps the spiritual experience sane.
When is it? When a philosopher writes about metaphysics. When you integrate a psychedelic experience into your daily life. When you build a theology.
Where is it? In the Translation Layer. It is the bridge between the infinite sky and the finite earth.
Why is it? For Communication and Sanity. Pure transcendence is silence. To share it, to live it, you must ground it in logical structures (language, ethics, practice).
How does it operate? Through Metaphor and Structure. It builds logical frameworks (like this Ontology) to house and point towards the transcendent truth.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Rational Mysticism. It asserts that while the ultimate truth may be super-rational, it is not anti-rational, and logic serves as the necessary basecamp for the ascent.
