25. Logic :: Describes (Existence)
Who is it? The Scientist. The Taxonomist. The Observer. It is the eye that looks at the world and sees patterns, not magic.
What is it? The Quantification of Reality. Existence is; Logic explains. Logic describes existence through physics, chemistry, and math. It turns the mystery of the universe into an equation.
When is it? Whenever we measure, categorize, or define something. When we write a law of physics (E=mc^2).
Where is it? In the Model. It is the map of the territory. It lives in textbooks, blueprints, and databases.
Why is it? For Comprehension and Mastery. We cannot control what we cannot describe. By describing existence logically, we can predict it and manipulate it (technology).
How does it operate? Through Pattern Recognition and Labeling. It observes phenomena, identifies the repeating rule, and assigns a symbol to it.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Epistemological Mapping. It is the process of translating the raw, analog data of reality into the digital, logical structure of knowledge.
