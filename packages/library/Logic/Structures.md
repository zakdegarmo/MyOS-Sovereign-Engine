21. Logic :: Structures (Self)
Who is it? The Architect. The Bone Carver. The Organizer. It is the internal framework that stops the Self from being a chaotic puddle of feelings. It is the skeleton of the Ego.
What is it? The Coherence of Identity. Without logic, the Self would be schizophrenic—believing it is a king one moment and a toaster the next. Logic structures the Self into a consistent narrative: "I am [Name], born in [Year], doing [Job]."
When is it? When you tell your life story, when you defend your actions, or when you maintain your sanity during a crisis. It acts continuously to keep the timeline of "You" straight.
Where is it? In the Autobiographical Memory. It resides in the orderly filing system of your history and your defined traits.
Why is it? For Stability and Integrity. A structured Self can withstand pressure. A structured Self is predictable to itself and others, which is necessary for trust and long-term planning.
How does it operate? Through Consistency Checks. It rejects data that doesn't fit the profile ("I wouldn't do that, that's not me"). It enforces a continuity of character.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Identity Architecture. It applies the rules of non-contradiction to the personality, ensuring the agent remains a singular, cohesive unit over time.
