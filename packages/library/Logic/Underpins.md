27. Logic :: Underpins (Mastery)
Who is it? The Theory. The Fundamentals. The Grid. It is the invisible grid lines behind the masterful drawing.
What is it? The Deep Structure of Skill. A master doesn't just guess; they internalize the logic of their craft so deeply they don't have to think about it. Logic is the physics behind the curveball, the harmony behind the jazz solo.
When is it? It is always present in high-level performance, even if invisible. It is the "Why" behind the "How."
Where is it? In the Subconscious Competence. It has moved from the conscious mind to the muscle memory, but it is still logic.
Why is it? For Reproducibility and Variation. If you understand the logic, you can do it again. If you understand the logic, you can break the rules creatively because you know why they exist.
How does it operate? Through Internalized Algorithms. It operates as the operating system upon which the application of "Style" runs.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Theoretical Substrate. It asserts that true mastery is not luck or magic, but the supreme internalization of the logical laws governing a specific domain.
