23. Logic :: Foundation (Logic)
Who is it? The Axiom. The Ground. The Tautology. It is the bedrock that says "A is A." It is the rule that justifies all other rules.
What is it? The Self-Validating Core. Logic cannot be proven by anything outside of itself (you need logic to prove logic). Therefore, its foundation is circular but solid. It is the set of First Principles.
When is it? Always. It is the timeless substrate of all reason. It is present in every valid equation and every sound argument.
Where is it? In the Abstract Realm. It exists in the fabric of mathematics and reality itself. It is not invented; it is discovered.
Why is it? For Universal Truth. Without a foundation, truth is relative and communication is impossible. If 2+2=5 for you and 4 for me, we cannot build a bridge. Logic provides the shared protocol.
How does it operate? Through Self-Evidence. It operates by being undeniably true. You cannot argue against the Law of Non-Contradiction without using the Law of Non-Contradiction.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Axiomatic Grounding. It is the recursive anchor point of the system, providing the immutable constants upon which all variable complexity is built.
