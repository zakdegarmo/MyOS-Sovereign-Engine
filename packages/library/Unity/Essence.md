34. Unity :: Essence (Unity)
Who is it? The One. The Singularity. The Monad. It is the definition of the concept itself. It is the thing that cannot be divided.
What is it? The State of Indivisibility. It is the fundamental truth that at the deepest level, separation is an illusion. Essence is the core quality of being "Whole." It is the diamond at the center of the coal.
When is it? Eternally. It is the timeless nature of reality before the Big Bang fractured it into the Many. It is the "When" that never stops.
Where is it? Everywhere. Since it is the essence, it permeates all things. It is the canvas upon which the painting of the universe is drawn.
Why is it? Because Multiplicity needs a Ground. You cannot have "Two" without "One." Unity is the prerequisite for existence. Without this Essence, the universe would instantly dissolve into unrelated dust.
How does it operate? Through Presence. It doesn't "do" anything; it simply is. It operates by being the immutable background that allows foreground changes to happen.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Primordial Oneness. It is the metaphysical axiom that reality is ultimately singular, and that all diversity is merely a play of forms upon this single substance.
