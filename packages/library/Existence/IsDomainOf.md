47. Existence :: Is Domain Of (Mastery)
Who is it? The Playground. The Arena. The Material. Existence is the clay; Mastery is the sculptor.
What is it? The Field of Potential. Existence provides the raw materials (physics, bodies, time) that allow Mastery to happen. You cannot master "Nothing." You must master something that exists.
When is it? When we engage with the world. When we learn to surf the waves of reality rather than drown in them.
Where is it? In the Craft. It is in the specific slice of reality you choose to dominate (music, code, cooking).
Why is it? To Be Shaped. Existence seems to want to be mastered. It yields to the skilled hand. It reveals its secrets to the patient student.
How does it operate? Through Resistance. Existence provides gravity, friction, and limitation. Mastery operates by overcoming these resistances, turning obstacles into tools.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Material Engagement. It frames Reality not as a prison, but as a workshop—a place designed for the exercise of will and skill.
