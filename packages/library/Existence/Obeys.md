43. Existence :: Obeys (Logic)
Who is it? The Subject. The Law-Abider. The Physical World. Existence is wild, but it is not lawless. It follows the rules.
What is it? The Constraint of Reality. Existence cannot do just anything. It cannot make a square circle. It cannot make 2+2=5. "Obeys" means that reality is bound by the structure of Logic/Physics.
When is it? Always. Every time an apple falls (Gravity), every time a chemical reaction balances, Existence is obeying Logic.
Where is it? In the Laws of Physics. It is written into the fabric of spacetime.
Why is it? For Order and Stability. If Existence did not obey Logic, the universe would be a chaotic nightmare where things popped in and out of being randomly. Life could not exist. Logic makes Existence habitable.
How does it operate? Through Causality and Consistency. It operates via the rule "Same cause, same effect." It ensures that the universe is reliable and predictable.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Natural Law. It asserts that Being is not arbitrary; it is structured. It implies that the universe is a Code that executes without error.
