11. Thought :: Informs (Self)
Who is it? The Messenger. The News Anchor. The Intelligence Officer. It is the stream of data reporting to the commander. It is the internal voice that says, "Sir, we have a situation."
What is it? The act of Data Delivery. Thought takes raw sensory input and abstract patterns and packages them into a format the Self can understand. It is the continuous briefing that updates the Self on the state of the world and its own internal condition.
When is it? Constantly. Every time you have a realization, a memory pops up, or you notice a sensation, Thought is informing the Self. It effectively stops only in deep sleep or unconsciousness.
Where is it? At the Interface of Awareness. It happens on the screen of the mind where the subconscious bubble ups into the conscious.
Why is it? For Situational Awareness. Without Thought informing the Self, the Self is a blind king. It needs intelligence to make decisions, to know where it is, and to know what is happening.
How does it operate? Through Language and Imagery. It encodes reality into symbols (words, pictures) and projects them onto the screen of awareness for the Self to review.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Cognitive Reporting. It establishes the feedback loop between the processor (Mind) and the user (Self), ensuring the agent is updated on the state of the simulation.
