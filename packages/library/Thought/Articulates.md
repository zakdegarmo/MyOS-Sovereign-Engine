18. Thought :: Articulates (Resonance)
Who is it? The Poet. The Translator. The Speaker. It is the part of the mind that struggles to put the ineffable feeling into words.
What is it? The Formulation of Expression. Resonance is a feeling (a vibe), but Thought gives it a body (a voice). It turns the internal hum into a broadcast signal.
When is it? When you write a love letter, compose a song, or finally find the words to explain why you are angry. When you translate an intuition into an argument.
Where is it? In the Larynx and the Pen. It sits at the output gate of the mind.
Why is it? For Transmission. You cannot share a feeling directly (telepathy doesn't exist yet). You must articulate it into a thought-form (language/art) to transfer it to another mind.
How does it operate? Through Vocabulary and Metaphor. It searches the database of language to find the closest symbolic match to the internal frequency.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Expressive Rendering. It is the conversion of analog internal states (feelings) into digital external signals (words/symbols) to facilitate connection and understanding.
