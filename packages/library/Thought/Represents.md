15. Thought :: Represents (Existence)
Who is it? The Cartographer. The Artist. The Simulator. It is the mind drawing a map of the territory. It is not the territory itself, but it’s the best drawing we have.
What is it? The creation of a Mental Model. Existence is too big and raw to hold in your head directly. Thought creates a symbol, a word, or an image to stand in for reality.
When is it? Whenever you use language, visualize an object, or remember a place. The word "Apple" is a thought representing the existing fruit.
Where is it? In the Virtual Sandbox. It resides in the simulation running inside the brain, which mirrors the physical world outside.
Why is it? For Prediction and Safety. If you can simulate a tiger in your head, you can plan how to escape it without actually having to face a real tiger. It allows for trial and error without physical consequence.
How does it operate? Through Symbolism and Abstraction. It strips away the infinite detail of reality and keeps only the relevant features, compressing the world into manageable bytes of data.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Symbolic Modeling. It acknowledges the gap between reality and the mind, and fills that gap with a user interface (language/concepts) that allows the system to interact with the world.
