20. Thought :: Transcends (Nothing/Everything)
Who is it? The Moth in the Flame. The Drop in the Ocean. It is the thought that thinks itself out of existence.
What is it? The Dissolution. It is the moment where the map (Thought) is recognized as distinct from the territory (Nothing/Everything), and the map is discarded. Thought realizes it is too small to hold the truth, so it surrenders.
When is it? In the final stage of meditation, or in the moment of death (ego death). When the chatter stops because there is nothing left to say.
Where is it? In the Silence. It is the space between thoughts that widens until it swallows the thinker.
Why is it? Because Thought is a Limit. Thought requires duality (thinker and object). To merge with the Nothing/Everything (Non-Duality), Thought must be transcended. You cannot think the Infinite; you can only be it.
How does it operate? Through Cessation. It operates by turning off. It is the system command exit(). It is the realization that the finger pointing at the moon is not the moon.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Cognitive Cessation. It is the ultimate function of the mind: to bring the user to the door of the Absolute, open it, and then stay behind.
