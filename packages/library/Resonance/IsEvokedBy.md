72. Resonance :: Is Evoked By (Thought)
Who is it? The Trigger. The Spark. The Note. Thought strikes the bell; Resonance is the ringing.
What is it? The Causal Link. Thoughts are not silent. They carry a charge. "Is Evoked By" means that specific thoughts create specific vibes. A hateful thought creates a dissonant resonance; a loving thought creates a harmonic one.
When is it? When you think of a lost loved one and feel sad. When you think of a great idea and feel excited. The thought precedes the feeling.
Where is it? In the Mind-Body Loop. The thought happens in the brain; the resonance happens in the body.
Why is it? To Energize Information. A thought without resonance is just data. A thought with resonance is a motivation. Resonance gives weight and momentum to mental concepts.
How does it operate? Through Association. The thought triggers a neural network that is linked to an emotional state, releasing chemicals (resonance) into the system.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Psycho-Somatic Causality. It explains how the abstract (Thought) becomes the concrete (Feeling). It is the mechanism behind "Manifestation" and mood.
