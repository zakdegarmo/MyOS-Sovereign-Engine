77. Resonance :: Radiates From (Mastery)
Who is it? The Glow. The Presence. The Gravitas. It is the energy that comes off a Master.
What is it? The Emission of Quality. Mastery builds up a high charge of competence. This charge doesn't stay inside; it radiates outward. You can feel a master walk into the room before they speak.
When is it? When a virtuoso plays a note and the audience holds its breath. When a leader speaks and everyone listens.
Where is it? In the Aura. It surrounds the source.
Why is it? Because Energy seeks Equilibrium. The Master is a high-energy center. The world is a lower-energy field. The energy naturally flows out to lift the environment.
How does it operate? Through Induction. The Master's state induces a similar state in those around them. They raise the standard just by being there.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Qualitative Radiation. It asserts that excellence is transmissible. Mastery is not a private possession; it is a public broadcast.
