66. Mastery :: Is Goal Of (Improvement)
Who is it? The Summit. The Trophy. The Destination. Mastery is the North Star that guides the ship of Improvement.
What is it? The Teleological End. Improvement is a vector (direction); Mastery is the point (location). We improve in order to master. Without Mastery as a goal, improvement is just aimless wandering.
When is it? It acts as a future attractor. It pulls us forward from the future.
Where is it? At the Asymptote. You get closer and closer, but maybe never fully touch it. It is the horizon.
Why is it? To Provide Direction. Goals organize behavior. The goal of Mastery organizes a lifetime of effort into a coherent narrative of growth.
How does it operate? Through Incentive. The vision of Mastery motivates the struggle of Improvement.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Teleological Attractor. It suggests that the ideal state (Mastery) exerts a gravitational pull on the current state, organizing our evolutionary path.
