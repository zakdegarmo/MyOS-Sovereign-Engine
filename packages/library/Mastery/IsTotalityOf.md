70. Mastery :: Is Totality Of (Nothing/Everything)
Who is it? The Circle. The Whole. The System. Mastery is not a part; it is the comprehension of the Whole.
What is it? The Holographic Grasp. The novice sees parts (the steering wheel, the brake). The master sees the totality (the car, the road, the physics). To master a thing is to hold the "Nothing and Everything" of that thing in your mind simultaneously.
When is it? When you "grok" the subject. When you know it inside and out, front and back.
Where is it? In the Gestalt. It is the forest, not the trees.
Why is it? For Control. You cannot control a system if you only understand 50% of it. Mastery requires the totality. You must accept the failures (Nothing) and the successes (Everything) of the domain.
How does it operate? Through Encompassment. The master wraps their mind around the entire domain, leaving nothing outside.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Domain Holism. It asserts that true mastery is comprehensive. It is the ability to hold the paradoxes and polarities of a field in a single, unified consciousness.
