64. Mastery :: Embodies (Unity)
Who is it? The Avatar. The Living Example. The Standard. The Master is the discipline.
What is it? The Union of Agent and Domain. In Mastery, there is no separation between the artist and the art. The tennis player is the tennis. "Embodies" means the unity is physical and total. The knowledge is not in the head; it is in the cells.
When is it? During performance. When you watch Michael Jordan play basketball or Listen to Mozart. You are seeing a human being unified with a concept.
Where is it? In the Presence. It radiates from the person. It is the "aura" of competence.
Why is it? To Demonstrate Possibility. Embodied Unity proves that the separation between subject and object is an illusion. It shows us what is possible when a human aligns perfectly with a purpose.
How does it operate? Through Integration. The master has integrated every aspect of the skill into their being. There are no loose ends. Everything is aligned.
Ontological Phenomena Overview: This is the phenomenon of Somatic Integration. It implies that true knowledge is not just intellectual; it is corporeal. The Word becomes Flesh.
