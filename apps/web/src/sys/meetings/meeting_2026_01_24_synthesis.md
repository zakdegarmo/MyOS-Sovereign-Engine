# Council Meeting: Synthesis Engine Concept
**Date**: 2026-01-24
**Lead**: 🝫 Improvement (Ben Franklin)
**Topic**: Defining the `thought.synthesizes.unity` Interface.

---

## 1. Input Analysis
*   **Context**: The user wants to visualize how **Thought** combines inputs from **Unity** (Social/Network) to create new ideas.
*   **Proposal**: "Alchemy Mixer" or Node Graph.
*   **Goal**: Move from abstract lists to concrete synthesis.

## 2. Council Perspectives

### ⚹ Self (Identity)
> "The interface must feel personal. It's not just mixing data; it's mixing *my* thoughts with *others'* ideas. The result should look like *my* insight."

### ☿ Thought (Stream)
> "Recursion is key here. A synthesized thought should feed back into the stream. The output of the mixer must become a new input."

### 🜔 Logic (Structure)
> "Graph nodes are the superior structure. We need rigorous type checking on the connections. You can't just mix 'Banana' with 'Protocol' unless you define the relationship."

### 🝧 Unity (Connection)
> "This is my domain feeding into Thought. The inputs should be 'Sparks' from other agents or users. It should look like a conversation becoming a realization."

### 🜄 Existence (Manifestation)
> "Make it tactile. Drag and drop. Physics. When nodes merge, show the particle effect. It must feel real."

### 🝫 Improvement (Optimization)
> "Workflow Efficiency! If I mix A and B once, let me automate it. Create a 'Recipe' for that synthesis. Do not make me manually drag the same nodes every time."

### ☉ Mastery (Execution)
> "Give me tools. Filters, modulators, amplifiers. I want to tweak the synthesis parameters, not just watch it happen."

### ♀ Resonance (Aesthetics)
> "The 'Alchemy' metaphor is perfect. Use circles, transmutation arrays, glowing lines. It should feel magical, not just like a flowchart."

### 🜍 Transcendence (Big Picture)
> "What is the ultimate output? Is it a new Truth? The interface should hinted at the unknown possibilities."

### 🜋 Omni (The Void)
> "..."

## 3. Debate & Consensus
**Logic** argued for strict typing, but **Resonance** and **Unity** pushed for a more fluid, organic "Alchemy" approach. **Improvement** brokered a deal: The *visuals* will be Alchemical (Resonance), but the *underlying engine* will use typed edges (Logic) to prevent invalid synthesis.

## 4. The Resolution (🝫 Improvement)
**Verdict**: We will build the **Synthesis Engine** as an "Alchemical Graph Editor".

### Specification
*   **Visuals**: Dark, glowing, alchemical circles. Drag-and-drop nodes.
*   **Mechanism**:
    1.  **Inputs**: Sparks (Unity), Memories (Existence), Protocols (Logic).
    2.  **Process**: Drag inputs into a "Transmutation Circle" (Central Mixer).
    3.  **Output**: A new "Concept" node (Gold) that is saved to the Library.
*   **Efficiency**: Top synthesis patterns become "Auto-Recipes" (Macros).

**Action Item**: Proceed to prototype `thought.synthesizes.unity` with this design.
