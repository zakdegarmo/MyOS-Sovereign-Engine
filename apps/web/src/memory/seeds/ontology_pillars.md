# The 10 Pillars of MyOntology
These pillars form the Fractal Semantic Genome of MyOS.

1. **Self (⚹)**: Identity, Agency, and the Core Observer.
2. **Thought (☿)**: Architecture, Analysis, and Mental Models.
3. **Logic (🜔)**: Truth, Kernels, and Structured Reasoning.
4. **Unity (🝧)**: Connection, Networking, and Cohesion.
5. **Existence (🜄)**: Assets, Physicality, and Grounding.
6. **Improvement (🜏)**: Optimization, Refinement, and Growth.
7. **Mastery (☉)**: Skills, Precision, and Peak Performance.
8. **Resonance (♀)**: Aesthetics, Vibes, and Emotional Alignment.
9. **Transcendence (🜍)**: Beyond the Grid, Deployment, and Evolution.
10. **Omni (🜋)**: Synthesis, The All, and The Nothing.
